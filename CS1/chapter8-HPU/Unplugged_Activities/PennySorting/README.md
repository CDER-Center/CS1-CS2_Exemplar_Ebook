The Penny Sorting Activity is an unplugged, hands-on classroom exercise that introduces introductory computer science students to fundamental PDC concepts through a collaborative sorting and searching task. Students act as processors and work individually or in parallel to identify the oldest penny from one or more collections of coins.
By comparing sequential execution with multiple parallel execution strategies, students gain an intuitive understanding of how workload distribution, processor coordination, and execution models influence performance. The activity requires a collection of pennies (or paper replicas) and minimal classroom materials, making it easy to implement in CS1 courses without requiring prior experience in parallel programming.
